# Bearly Technical

The website for Bearly Technical: websites, booking, Google listings and grant help for the small businesses, farms, churches and nonprofits of Black Mountain and the Swannanoa Valley, North Carolina.

Live: https://jrns-hub.github.io/bearly-technical/

- `index.html`: the whole site, one file, no build step.
- `lights.json`: the hero's valley lights, one per real local business or nonprofit with a preview site (from the call list leads).
- `img/`: the bear badge and screenshots of hand-built preview sites.

Preview sites live in https://github.com/jrns-hub/previews.
