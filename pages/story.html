Around the world, always local

Every place taught the same lesson: the small organizations do the big work.

Before Bearly Technical, there were years on the road, working beside the people who protect animals, feed neighbors and heal their towns. Now that same care comes home to a mountain town in North Carolina.

Places lived and worked · drag to spin

The road here

Nepal, Japan, Dubai, Portugal, Spain, France, Romania, Mexico and Hawaii, and every US state except Rhode Island and Alaska, from Los Angeles and the Bay Area to New York City. A home base in St. Augustine, Florida. Over a hundred communities lived in and learned from, and more than 15,000 hours volunteered.

Then Black Mountain: the first place in years with neighbors, clients and a reason to stay.

13 states

The free medicine bus

A nonprofit bus that carried free coffee and more than 10,000 servings of herbal medicine across thirteen states. Served on its board.

Leadership

Nonprofit boards

Four years on a nonprofit board, one of them as president, working its grants. That work became Grant Guru.

Land

The Nature Conservancy

Invasive plant work to give native forests room to breathe, plus an orchard in Hawaii and a landscaping crew built from volunteers who needed work.

Food

Food forests and free food

Started food forests to support free food distribution around the world, and helped distribute more than 4,000 pounds of food with local organizations.

Arts

Arts collectives

A few years managing an arts collective in Tennessee, and arts collective work in Asheville.

Care

Caregiving

Paid caregiving for elders through stroke recovery and dementia, and learning how to navigate the systems that are supposed to help.

Now

The Swannanoa Valley

113 preview sites, 77 grant reports and a growing network of neighbors, all in one October. Being the change, one town at a time.

The world is changed by small groups who keep showing up. Bearly Technical exists to keep them showing up.
