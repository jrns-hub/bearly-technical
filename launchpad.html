The path, streamlined

Six phases. Skip what you already have. Most people go from first call to taking payments in two to four weeks.

Phase 01

Check the idea

Who it is for, what they pay now, what you will charge, and the smallest version you can sell next week.

  • One-page plan you can show a bank or a partner
  • Simple price and cost math
Phase 02

Name, logo and brand

A name people remember, a logo that reads small on a phone, colors and a voice that sound like you.

  • Name and domain check
  • Logo, colors and a one-line pitch
Phase 03

Legal and money setup

The paperwork in the right order, walked through together: business registration with the state, an EIN, a business bank account, and sales tax if you need it.

  • Checklist for North Carolina, step by step
  • Where an accountant or lawyer is worth it, said plainly
Phase 04

Open online

A real website, a Google listing, a business email, and a way to get paid, all connected.

  • Website with booking or ordering
  • Square or Stripe payments, invoices, a QR code for the counter
Phase 05

First customers

A launch plan built on the town itself: introductions to neighboring businesses, flyers with a QR code, a listing in the valley network, and your first reviews.

  • Launch week plan
  • Warm intros through the Bearly network
Phase 06

Systems that run themselves

The quiet machinery: a customer list, simple bookkeeping, reminders, and AI helpers that answer the common questions, so you spend your hours on the work you love.

  • Bookkeeping set up in Wave or QuickBooks
  • Automations and an AI helper trained on your own answers
